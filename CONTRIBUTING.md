# Contributing

Submit a source URL and exact inscription/page/line locator with every correction.
Separate source transcription, reading proposal, Greek interpretation and translation.
State whether you inspected a primary publication, object/image, or secondary source.
Do not upload restricted scans or images without a redistribution licence.

Do not edit raw XML to make it agree with project expectations. For source changes,
retain prior snapshots in git and update acquisition hashes, accession dates and
modification notes. Rebuild, validate, test and regenerate exports before publishing.
Report classification disagreements explicitly; do not silently promote uncertain
records into the Greek subset. No independent reviewer credit without their review.
