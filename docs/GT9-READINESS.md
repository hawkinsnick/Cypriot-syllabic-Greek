# >9 readiness and critical frontier

Cypriot Syllabic Greek now has method-parity controls around a substantial licensed first-fascicle layer: 593 IG XV 1,1 source responses, 592 parsed digital records and one preserved malformed-source quarantine. The digital edition is CC BY 4.0. These are edition entries, not a certified physical-object count.

The collection-wide corpus is much larger. The scholarly corpus project historically reported about 1,397 inscriptions and planned multiple geographic fascicles; IG XV 1,1 covers Amathus, Kourion and Marion. Paphos and other regions therefore remain an explicit expansion frontier rather than an invisible completeness gap.

Masson/ICS, Egetmeyer and modern IG are source lineages, not automatic independent witnesses. Coin types, specimens, numbered parts and objects remain separate units. Modern autopsy-based revisions must be represented as source-attributed disagreements.

Engineering parity is complete when API/export/validation/CI are wired. A defensible >9 scholarly score still requires substantially broader authoritative coverage and object/edition concordance; those gains depend on publication/source access and, eventually, expert epigraphic review.
