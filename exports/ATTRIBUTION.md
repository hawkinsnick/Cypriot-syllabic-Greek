Cypriot syllabic Greek research corpus — source attribution and reuse

Raw XML in data/raw/ig and exports/tei and derived edition content in
data/records.json and exports are adapted from Inscriptiones Graecae,
Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities (BBAW), TELOTA:
https://telota.bbaw.de/ig/

Printed scholarly edition: Artemis Karnava and Massimo Perna, with Markus
Egetmeyer, Inscriptiones Graecae XV, Pars I, Fasciculus 1: Inscriptiones
Amathontis, Curii, Marii (Berlin, 2020). The digital XML's own bibliographical
wording is retained in each record. Digital translations retain their language
and responsibility fields, including Klaus Hallof where identified. Blank
responsibility fields are unknown, not project authorship.

Each source XML states that reuse/distribution must link to its original page.
Each JSON record and CSV catalogue row includes this link. Retain this NOTICE
and the source links when redistributing exports or derived records. No BBAW
endorsement, independent expert review, or exhaustive coverage is claimed.

Modifications: XML parsed into JSON; readable whitespace and part boundaries
normalized; classifications and conservative token indexes added. Raw XML is
redistributed unchanged. Greek alphabetic interpretations are kept separate
from syllabic transliterations and modern translations. No photographs, plate
drawings, or scans of the printed corpus are redistributed.
